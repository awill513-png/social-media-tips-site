Our story

Why this site exists

Every app has an insights tab, but very few people know what to do with it. Numbers like "reach," "impressions" and "engagement rate" sound technical, and most advice online is either too vague or tries to sell you something.

Social Pulse was created to fix that. It's a simple, honest guide to social media analytics plus practical posting tips you can use today, whether you're a student, a creator, a small business or managing a brand's account.
