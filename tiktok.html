TikTok tips that keep people watching.

How it works

What TikTok rewards

Follower count matters less on TikTok than anywhere else. Every video gets a fair shot, and these signals decide how far it goes.

Watch time

Did people watch the whole thing, or even twice? This is the #1 signal.

Shares

Sending a video to a friend is one of the strongest signals that it's worth showing to more people.

Comments

Conversation keeps a video alive and gives you ideas for reply videos.

Search match

TikTok is a search engine. Keywords you say and show on screen help people find you.

Example retention curves: the percent of viewers still watching over time.

Read your retention graph

The first 2 seconds decide everything

In TikTok Studio, open any video's analytics and look at the retention curve. A steep early drop means your hook needs work. A drop in the middle means the pacing got slow.

  • Aim for more than 70% still watching at the 3-second mark.
  • A bump near the end means people rewatched. That's great!
  • Compare your 5 best videos' curves to find your winning pace.

Copy & paste

6 hook formulas that work

Say it out loud and put it on screen in the first second.

Mistake

"Stop doing ___ if you want ___."

Fear of missing out on a mistake makes people stay.

Result first

"Here's how I got ___ in ___ days."

Show the end result, then explain how you got there.

List

"3 ___ nobody tells you about."

Numbers tell viewers exactly how long they need to stay.

POV

"POV: you finally ___."

Puts the viewer inside a moment they relate to.

Hot take

"Unpopular opinion: ___."

A bold claim gets people commenting, both agreeing and disagreeing.

Curiosity

"I tested ___ so you don't have to."

People want the answer, so they keep watching to the end.

Anatomy of a great TikTok

Structure your video like this

✂️ Cut the dead air

Remove every "um," every breath, every pause. Jump cuts keep the pace up.

🔁 Make it loop

If the last line flows back into the first, people rewatch without noticing.

🔤 Always add captions

Many people scroll with the sound off. On-screen text also helps your video show up in search.

Posting tips

Tips & tricks for every upload

  • Length: 7–20 seconds is easiest to watch all the way through. Go longer only if you're telling a story.
  • Sounds: Use trending audio at low volume under your own voice.
  • Keywords: Say them, show them on screen, and put them in the caption.
  • Hashtags: 3–5 specific ones. Skip #fyp, which doesn't help.
  • Volume: Post 1–3 times a day while you're testing. More posts means faster learning.
  • Replies: Turn great comments into "reply with video" posts.
  • Series: Make "Part 1, Part 2…" series so viewers come back for more.
  • Cover: Pick a readable cover frame so your profile grid looks organized.

Quick guide

Do this, not that

✅ Do

  • Start with the most interesting moment
  • Film vertically (9:16) in good light
  • Reply to comments in the first hour
  • Join trends early and add your own twist
  • Check your analytics weekly and adjust

🚫 Don't

  • Open with "Hey guys, so today…"
  • Repost videos with another app's watermark
  • Delete videos that flop (it can hurt your account)
  • Use #fyp or dozens of off-topic hashtags
  • Give up after 5 videos, because TikTok rewards volume
