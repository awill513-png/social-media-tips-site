How it works

What Instagram rewards

Instagram has said that sends (shares in DMs), watch time and likes are among its biggest ranking signals. Saves show your content is worth keeping.

💌 Sends

Shares in DMs are the top signal for reaching people who don't follow you. Make content people want to send to a friend.

⏱️ Watch time

For Reels, how long people watch and whether they rewatch.

🔖 Saves

Tutorials, checklists and tips get saved. Saves show Instagram that a post has lasting value.

❤️ Likes & comments

These matter most for reaching your existing followers. Reply to keep the conversation going.

Choose your format

What each format is best for

Use each format for its strength. A good mix is Reels to grow, carousels to teach and Stories to connect.

General strengths of each format, rated 1–5. Use your own insights to see what works for your audience.

Reels

Reels: your growth engine

  • Hook in 1–2 seconds with motion and bold on-screen text.
  • Keep it short. Under 30 seconds is easier to rewatch, but longer works if it holds attention.
  • Leave room for the interface. Keep text away from the bottom and right edges.
  • Make it "sendable." Relatable moments and useful tips get shared in DMs.
  • Post original content. Reposts with another app's watermark get less reach.
  • Choose a cover that looks good in the 3:4 profile grid crop.
  • Try Trial Reels to test an idea on non-followers first.

Reel safe zones (9:16, 1080×1920)

Carousels

Anatomy of a carousel people save

Carousels can get a second chance in the feed. If someone skips the post, Instagram may show it to them again starting on slide 2, so make slide 2 a strong hook as well.

📐 Use 4:5 size

1080×1350 takes up more of the feed than square posts.

🔤 Big, readable text

About 30 words per slide at most. If you have to squint, it's too small.

➡️ Add a swipe cue

An arrow or a cut-off element at the edge encourages people to swipe.

🎵 Add music

Photo carousels with audio can also appear in the Reels feed.

The extras

Captions, hashtags & Stories

✍️ Captions

  • The first line is a second hook. Don't waste it.
  • Use keywords people search for. Instagram search reads captions.
  • End with a question or a clear call-to-action.
  • Short line breaks make captions easier to read.

# Hashtags

  • Use 3–5 relevant tags. Instagram now caps posts at 5 hashtags.
  • Pick specific tags (#smallbizmarketing), not huge ones (#love).
  • Keywords in your caption matter more than hashtags now.
  • Check the Hashtags line in a post's insights to see if they bring reach.

⭕ Stories

  • Post daily to stay at the front of your followers' Story bar.
  • Polls, quizzes and question stickers boost replies.
  • Share new posts to Stories to give them a push.
  • Save your best Stories to Highlights on your profile.

Instagram insights

What to check after every post

Tap "View insights" under any post (you'll need a Professional account). Wait 48–72 hours before judging a post.

  • Views & accounts reached: How far did it go? Check the percentage of non-followers.
  • Sends (shares): Your best sign of reaching new people.
  • Saves: Shows your content is useful enough to keep.
  • Average watch time (Reels): Compare it to the Reel's length.
  • Skip rate (Reels): How many people swiped away in the first 3 seconds.
  • Profile activity & follows: Did the post turn viewers into followers?

Quick guide

Do this, not that

✅ Do

  • Keep a consistent look and color palette
  • Reply to every comment in the first hour
  • Post Stories daily, even simple ones
  • Collaborate using the Collab post feature
  • Repurpose your top carousels as Reels

🚫 Don't

  • Buy followers (it kills your engagement rate)
  • Use more than 5 or unrelated hashtags
  • Post TikToks with the watermark still on
  • Ask for likes with "like if you agree" bait
  • Judge a post in the first hour
